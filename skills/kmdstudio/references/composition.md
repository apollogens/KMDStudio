# Composition decisions

| Card and occasion | Decision and supported controls | Avoid | Verify |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D01 Dominant gesture, on an opening page | Give one image, title, question or `highlight` the most space or contrast. Use `cover.image_position`, `flow`, `display_pt` or a lead. Align smaller elements with the focal region. | Title, banner and metric competing for first attention. | The first focal point reads at thumbnail size. |
| D02 Asymmetric anchor, for short metadata near prose | Use a narrow `sidebar` rail at 25–40% and one short prose block in the wider region. Use serial paragraphs for long text or linear navigation. | Equal regions for unequal reading tasks. | Rail values wrap cleanly. Native order starts with the rail. |
| D03 Editorial sequence, for several sections | Plan distinct entry, explanation, evidence, transition and action roles. Sequence native blocks and use `heading.new_page` only at a real change of task. | Identical arrangements on consecutive sections or a break before a continuation. | Contact sheets reveal roles. No heading, caption or fragment is isolated. |
| D06 Controlled color field, at a meaningful transition | Use a level-one `heading` with `treatment: panel` or a single `highlight`. Prefer `tone: plain` when surrounding elements already create contrast. | A colored box for every statement. | The field separates tasks, with legible text and a discernible hierarchy in grayscale. |
| D07 Deliberate space, for a quiet or image-led page | Use `spacing.margin_mm`, `paragraph_pt` and `component_pt` to keep related blocks close and leave room between groups. Use `cover.flow: continue` when the cover can share a page. | A blank spill page, an orphan caption or compressed type to satisfy a page count. | At full-page scale the intervals identify groups and every page has a reading purpose. |
| D11 Transaction clarity, for obligations and dates | Group short date, identity and reference fields in `sidebar` or serial text. Keep amounts and units in `table` with right-aligned values. Put the action in a clear `heading` and paragraph. | Ornament louder than the action, or a total embedded in artwork. | Scan party, due date and action. Recalculate amounts independently. |
| D13 Shared grid, for a publication with varied page roles | Reuse page margins and a text edge. Vary widths through `figure.width_mm`, `figure.image_percent`, `sidebar_percent`, contextual full-width tables or an eligible `layout.columns` prose range. | Using every layout device or changing alignment without a reading reason. | At thumbnail scale the same axis holds sections together and the focal points still differ. |
| D15 Same source, when exploring alternative concepts | Change opening, focal hierarchy and region relationships through cover flow, image placement and native block treatment. Change type scale or image width as a consequence of the concept. | Swapping only fonts or colors. | Compare anonymous page sheets. Verify native text and assets against the frozen source. |

## Original composition studies

The sketches show reading order and approximate relationships. `|` denotes adjacent regions, `[ ]` a reading band and `→` an optional page or section transition. Word reflows each band.

| Study and sketch | Content, focus and alignment | Proportions, density and image role | Native implementation and fallback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence-first · `[title and decision] → [key measure] [comparison] [action]` | A short report. The first finding or decision dominates. Align its lead and the numeric comparison to the same text edge. | Broad evidence region, compact context and actions. An illustration can situate the decision. | Continuing cover, plain `highlight`, full-width `table.bars`, then action heading. Use aligned values and text if the graphic adds little. |
| Quiet agreement · `[title] [date rail | terms] [fees] [reply]` | A booking, confirmation or agreement. The title anchors the upper left. The next action has the clearest secondary emphasis. | Roughly 30:70 for short labels and prose, compact fees, generous outer space, no required image. | `sidebar`, data `table` and native heading. Use serial labeled text with the same meaning when the rail is too narrow. |
| Editorial lesson · `[objective] [worked model] [trace] → [listing] [practice and feedback]` | A worked technical example. The model and trace share a left edge. A new region signals the change from understanding to applying. | Sparse opening, denser full-width technical region. An explanatory image belongs next to its step. | Lead, `diagram`, `table`, `code`, `exercise` and feedback paragraph. Substitute ordered steps when diagram labels grow. |
| Exhibition sequence · `[large art] [entry] → [context | detail] → [index] → [view A] [view B]` | A catalogue or field guide. Art leads the entry, a study leads close looking, then the shared edge and caption baseline connect views. | Wide opening, wider prose in context, denser index and same-scale comparisons. Images show the detail the reader will compare. | Illustrated `cover`, paired `figure`, native table and serial equal-width figures. Use ordinary serial figures and prose if narrow regions or navigation become difficult. |
| Type-led interpretation · `[large title] [short lead] [small art] → [field heading] [comparison] [action]` | The same evidence as an image-led report. Type establishes entry. A section field signals the shift to evidence and actions share a consistent left edge. | Strong display with compact metadata. Art is a supporting detail, followed by a denser table and a short action region. | `cover.image_position: after-lead`, display scale, selective `heading` panel and compact paired art. Use a plain heading and serial figure when the transition or pair is too strong. |
